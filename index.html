How the garden works

Araneus vitae, the cross-backed life spider

  • Cells follow Conway's rules, except no cell is born on silk, and a living cell that lands on silk is caught and cannot die.
  • Spiders hunt cells within 7 squares and feel any caught cell within 24. Each meal restores energy.
  • Idle spiders spin orb webs in widening spirals. Silk fades after about 250 generations.
  • A well-fed spider lays a new spider. A starving one dies, and its body seeds a patch of new cells.
